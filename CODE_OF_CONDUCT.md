# Code of Conduct

Memi contributors are expected to make technical and design collaboration safe, specific, and constructive.

- Critique the work and evidence, not the person.
- Respect accessibility needs, identity, experience level, and cultural context.
- Do not harass, threaten, dox, or publish private information.
- Do not use community spaces for spam, deceptive promotion, or manufactured engagement.
- Preserve attribution and respect upstream maintainers.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact path at [memoire.cv](https://memoire.cv).
