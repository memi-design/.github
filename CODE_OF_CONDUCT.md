# Code of Conduct

Memi contributors are expected to make technical and design collaboration safe,
specific, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the work and evidence, not the person.
- Respect accessibility needs, identity, experience level, and cultural context.
- Give and accept feedback with clarity, patience, and good faith.
- Preserve attribution and respect upstream maintainers.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, sexualized attention, or discriminatory speech.
- Doxing or publishing another person's private information.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal attacks.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, impersonation, or manufactured engagement.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in organization repositories, issues, Discussions, pull
requests, release channels, and public events where someone represents Memi.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct on GitHub, use GitHub's private
[report-abuse path](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). To request
maintainer follow-up, use the private contact route documented in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) without posting sensitive details. Include links,
dates, context, and any immediate safety concern only through that private
channel. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports, limit access to those who need to respond,
and avoid conflicts of interest. Depending on severity and history, responses
may include a private correction, content removal, a warning, temporary
restriction, or permanent removal from community spaces. Reports made in bad
faith may themselves violate this policy.

If a report concerns a maintainer, that maintainer will not participate in the
decision. Retaliation is prohibited.
